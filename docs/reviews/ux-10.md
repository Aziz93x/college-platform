- Range, in run order: Steps 1 to 6, Step 41 (no new screens), Step 7 (settings), Step 8 (departments and the college), Step 9 (roles), Step 10 (people, accounts, profile).

## Verdict

**The screens are calm, right to left throughout, and every route has its states. The phone shell fails section 4's 200% text bar, five fixes raise the clarity and finish scores under 4, and seven consistency breaks, which explain every consistency score under 4, need fixing in this PR.**

What holds:

- Logical properties are used everywhere I looked.
- Icons that point are mirrored: sign out, the sidebar toggle, the breadcrumb chevron.
- The stepper and the slider are mirrored, so lower values sit at the start.
- Emails are in `<bdi>`. Secrets and file types are in LTR runs. Arabic plurals are right ("شخص واحد، شخصان، 3 أشخاص، 40 شخصًا").
- Refusals (403) and missing records (404) stay inside the shell.
- Partial states hide what a viewer cannot do: a head's people, settings per key, the system role.
- Reduced motion is honoured. Measured: the sidebar's entrance goes from 0.5 s to 0 s.
- The settings search folds Arabic spellings.
- Step 41's double identifier field did not come back in 50 loads.

What must change:

- **Clarity or finish under 4 (fixes F1 to F5):**
  - the phone shell at 320 px and at 200% text;
  - a role's grid, whose Save appears 1,886 px below the switch;
  - Today for an admin;
  - a person's page, whose account actions sit behind an unlabelled "…";
  - the settings stepper and slider finish.
- **Consistency breaks (C1 to C7), behind every consistency score under 4:**
  - two button shapes;
  - three save patterns for edit forms;
  - two search and no-match patterns;
  - access removed without a confirmation;
  - two-factor turned on outside the shell but off inside it;
  - "موقوف" used for three different states;
  - a design catalog that shows the components no screen uses.

## Decisions this review was asked to make

1. **The 390 px capture scale: CSS scale.**
   - At the iPhone's 3x, the 35 phone captures weighed 12 MB. At CSS scale they weigh 3.2 MB.
   - All three widths then share one scale, and a 44 px target measures 44 px in the image.
   - Change `e2e/screens/capture.screens.ts`: add `scale: "css"` to `page.screenshot()`. The phone still renders as a phone (touch, coarse pointer).
2. **Step 41's double identifier field.**
   - Found and fixed in Step 7. When Motion's features arrived while a streamed part waited for its reveal, React drew the part again beside itself. `revealsSettled()` (`src/components/motion/motion-provider.tsx`) now holds the features back, and `e2e/streaming.spec.ts` proves that one hydrated field remains.
   - Here, a MutationObserver over 50 loads of the production login page counted at most one `input[name="identifier"]` each time.
   - Remove the note from the checkpoint.
3. **Step 7's settings choices.**
   - Descriptions under the labels: keep. They read without a tap.
   - One Save per setting: keep. The values are independent. But bring the Save into view on a phone (F5).
   - The password asked in the row: keep. The grid, the members and the person's forms do the same.
   - Arabic matching:
     - "كلمه المرور" (haa for taa marbuta) finds 2 settings;
     - "اقصر" (no hamza) finds 1;
     - "الاسبوع" finds 6;
     - it misses Arabic-Indic digits ("٣٠" finds 0) and synonyms ("قفل" finds 0).
     - The search is good enough. Pass the query through `toLatinDigits` as the people search does.
   - `ui/switch`, `ui/select` and `ui/tabs` leave the catalog (C7).
4. **Step 8's choices.**
   - The Rayat values left off the phone list: keep. The department's page shows them.
   - The college's names under the list: keep. Say where they are in the platform name's description (S4).
   - One Save for a department against one per setting: keep, as a rule. A record saves together; independent values save alone (C2 writes it down).
   - The stepper's dashed outline: fix (F5).
   - The translucent sticky header: fix (F1).
5. **Step 9's choices.**
   - One column of switches per role: keep, with a Save that stays in view (F2).
   - The members' combobox and scope: keep.
   - The password in the card: keep.
   - Page order with up and down buttons: keep, and mark which pages become phone tabs (S5).
6. **Step 10's choices.**
   - The "…" in each row: keep.
   - On the person's own page, show the actions as buttons (F4).
   - The import's three stages on one page: keep. A stage line would help (S5).
   - Two forms on a person's page: keep, without repeating their values above them (F4).
   - The profile: keep, with two-factor turned on inside the shell (C5).
   - The phone list without the email and the roles: keep.
7. **One component per job (docs/design/beui-map.md, docs/BACKLOG.md line 19).**
   - Remove the Step 4 primitives that lost their job, with `cmdk`, `vaul` and `react-day-picker`, and show the canonical components on `/design` (C7).
   - Step 13 can ask the date range picker for a one-day range.
8. **Toasts: keep Sonner.**
   - It is tokenized, announced in its own region, and loaded only by the layouts that toast.
   - BeUI's toast stack would add script and a second stack beside the bell's.
   - Move it clear of the tab bar (F1).
9. **The desktop's largest paint (lead review A).**
   - On `/today` at 1440 px it is now the page header's lazy wave image, not the sidebar art. Measured: `IMG …element-wave.webp&w=1080`.
   - On a phone it is the banner text, which is what section 4 measures.
   - Removing the wave from the page header (S3) settles it.
