**The range's screens are complete, right to left and inside the shell, and every route has its states. The plan editor and the shared item sheet carry the range's hardest jobs well. Seven fixes raise the clarity and finish scores under 4, and eight consistency breaks need fixing in this PR. With F3 and F4, those breaks explain every consistency score under 4.**

### Must fix (under 4, or a consistency break)

- **Evidence.**

    - `/terms/new` scrolls 121 px sideways and `/terms/<id>` 120 px (`facts/sweep-vice-390.json`, `states/term-text-200-390`).
    - The fieldset keeps `w-[13.5rem]`, which is 432 px at 200%, with a fieldset's default `min-inline-size: min-content`.
    - So the term form's single grid column (`term-form.tsx:135`) and the periods' rows (`exam-periods.tsx:220`) grow to 432 px.

**Found while fixing.**

- **The sheet's toast covered its actions.** F5 put the item sheet's actions at the screen's foot, where the "حُفظ البند" toast sits. After "حفظ وإضافة آخر" the toast took the next click, and stayed while the pointer rested on it, as Sonner's toasts do. The 50-item run stalled on its second item. While the sheet stays open for the next item, the toast now shows at the top of the screen. It still names the weeks the item lands in, as Step 19 asks. `e2e/plan.spec.ts` checks the next "حفظ وإضافة آخر" can be clicked at once.
- **An axe check depended on the scroll.** After Save scrolled the exam periods into view, the sticky header covered the top of a period's row. Axe counted the bell and the account button over its trash against the trash's size ("partially obscured … 36px by 13.3px"). `seriousViolations()` now runs from the top of the page, as it loads.
- **Tests that read the base seed's settings** expect the new `navigation.order`: `tests/integration/seed-demo.test.ts` and `settings.test.ts`.
- **The transfer sheet's list stayed where its field had been.** The sheet focuses its trainer picker as it slides in, and the picker opens its list. The list followed scrolls and resizes, but not a field moved by the sheet's transform. So it could stay over the field: the re-capture found it there at 1440 px, while the first capture had caught it below. While open, a list now follows its field frame by frame, and a closed one follows nothing (`lib/popover-position.ts`, recorded in `PROVENANCE.md`). `e2e/design.spec.ts` moves the catalog's combobox by a transform and expects its list to follow. Without the change the list stayed 167 px away.
