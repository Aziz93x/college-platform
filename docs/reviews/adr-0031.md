# Review of ADR-0031: the head delegates the review and the portfolios' approval

- **What was reviewed:** ADR-0031 as first accepted (`88950e3`), with its notes under Steps 29 and 30, against the code at `b90ad39`.
- **Why:** the owner's rule of 23 October 2026, «تأكد من استخدام منطق صحيح 100%، راجع وجرب واختبر بعدة وكلاء».
- **How:** several agents on 23 and 24 October 2026:
  - a prover checked the record's 29 claims (C1 to C29) on the real services with 55 checks. Its integration test, `tests/integration/assignment-grant.test.ts`, keeps the ones the delegations build on;
  - two reviewers looked for gaps (F01 to F20, and F1 to F11);
  - a skeptic re-derived each claim and each finding from the code, and said whether it holds.
- **The outcome:** the owner answered six more questions on 24 October, and ADR-0031 was amended the same day.

## The claims

The grant works as ADR-0031 says (C1, C3 to C5, C7 to C14, C25 and C28):

- an assignment whose template grants a role gives it in the department for the term;
- revoking it removes the membership it added;
- a term's membership counts only while its term is active;
- a key held in a department authorizes that department only;
- only the seed sets the role a template grants.

The claims that did not hold as written are among the findings below: C2, C16 to C24, C26, C27 and C29.

## Not taken, with the reason

- **A log of who opened an exam copy (row 11):** the owner did not ask for it. The circle is the head and the people the head names in one department, and each is named with every decision.
- **The authority recorded on each decision (row 17):** the grant's events already say who held the delegation, and when.
- **A cap on the number of delegates (row 20):** the owner allowed several delegates.
- **A sample delegates cannot predict (row 16):** the sample takes finished work, so knowing it changes nothing.

## After the review

The owner then allowed an exam copy's sample in the portfolio (answers 11 and 12): its trainer uploads it with the copy, and every reader of the portfolio sees it once the copy is approved. Asked what a portfolio shows when the sample is missing, they set the rule for every axis (ADR-0034).
